Cardboard Innovations

A project

About the Project

For this project, I used cardboard to solve four different problems or needs. I tried to come up with simple and creative ways to make cardboard useful in everyday situations.

The Idea

My idea was to address different problems that could be solved using cardboard. I wanted to make things that were simple but still had a useful purpose.

What I Did

I created four different objects out of cardboard: a cup, glasses, a chair, and a knife. Each object was designed to solve a different problem, such as helping someone drink water, see the board better, rest their legs, or put condiments on a bagel.
