Arduino Reaction Time Game

Project Overview

For my Arduino final project, I made a reaction-time game. The game uses LEDs, a button, and a display to measure how quickly someone can react. The Arduino records the time between the start of the game and when the button is pressed, then shows the reaction time on the display.

1. Why I Chose This Project

I chose this project because I wanted to build something interactive instead of just having an LED turn on and off. I had already worked with LEDs and buttons, so I wanted to combine those skills with a display and use them to make something that actually responds to the user.

5. Technical Tidbit: How Reaction Time Is Measured

The Arduino measures reaction time by recording a starting time and then recording another time when the player presses the button. It subtracts the two times to find the amount of time the player took to react.

GAME STARTS ↓ Starting time recorded ↓ Player waits ↓ Button pressed ↓ Button press time recorded ↓ Times are subtracted ↓ Reaction time calculated ↓ Result shown on display
